Welcome to Memory Match! A game where two players try to match cards for the most points.

● Two players must take turns in selecting a pair of cards.

● The player playing their turn can only try one pair.

● If the current player gets a pair right, they score a point and get another turn in the row they played in.

● Gameplay goes until all cards have been paired and there are no cards to select.

● The player with the most points wins.
